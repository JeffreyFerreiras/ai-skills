# Visual and motion direction

Use a restrained dark slate/chalkboard world with icy cyan and lavender and limited semantic accents. Context scenes may use polished isometric forms; every object need not be chalk. For diagrams, preserve the useful topology and add shallow thickness, separated cards and short soft shadows. Choose recognizable role-specific silhouettes rather than generic cuboids. Keep smooth antialiased contours and crisp text.

The independent [visual "diva" reviewer](visual-diva-reviewer.md) holds every scene to a high art-direction bar: striking, attractive composition and polished assets/transitions while protecting comprehension. Maintain scene IDs/timecodes and coverage across keyframes, actual temporal inspection and encoded phone/master evidence. Reject mediocre generic blocks and camera-only animation where visible internal action is needed; use focused improvements, not indiscriminate decorative complexity. Beauty is a creative judgment, not an objective test score. Share the existing three-round scene/asset deliverable cap across reviewers and escalate material unresolved visual findings at the cap.

Create fresh episode-specific artwork for each new video. Reuse tools, rendering code, palette and design language, not recycled artwork from previous episodes. Preserve accepted art within the current episode during focused revisions; any explicit user request to reuse an asset takes precedence. Record provenance and source dimensions for current assets. Rebuild or author crisp replacements for soft old bitmaps rather than treating upscaling as recovered detail.

Before scene assembly, validate source pixel width/height, the actual cropped pixel region, its largest projected footprint during camera/actor motion and crop magnification. Compare available crop pixels with required rendered pixels; a large overall image does not guarantee a tiny enlarged crop is sharp. Preserve aspect ratio through transforms, use an intentional crop rather than stretching, and inspect the largest on-screen view at source and delivery resolution. Rasterize vector assets at the required footprint instead of enlarging a small cached raster; native vectors alone do not establish attractive artwork.

Inspect coherent geometry: aligned faces/edges, consistent perspective or isometric axes, plausible proportions, symmetric objects where intended, stable silhouettes and clean joins. Check transformed poses and transitions as well as the final still. Correct distorted or lopsided objects by repairing the source geometry, not hiding them with blur, glow or camera movement. Preserve approved audio/cues and timing while doing visual-quality work.

Each explanation needs a composition that exposes its mechanism. Alternate context, workbench, lane, tree, journey or comparison views as appropriate. Keep one scenario and consistent identities. Camera movement supplements meaningful internal action; panning a static image does not establish animation. Preserve stable teaching holds and level labels/readouts. Brief angled entrances can reveal depth, then settle to a readable view.

Keep only the title, scene/diagram, necessary labels and highlighted subtitles. Avoid decorative dashboards, progress strips, unrelated particles and phase cards. Do not copy UI overlays, watermarks or reference artwork. Record licenses for fonts and assets made for the production.

Animate real causal events: transfer a copy while retaining its source, compare values, change state after an event, assemble a dependency, or reconstruct an outcome. Make actors reach/type/tap, screens update and LEDs react when relevant. Pose crossfades are layered animation, not skeletal rigging. Quiet beeps accompany a few meaningful events; speech stays dominant. Constant flicker and random sounds add distraction.

Use fine curved paths with clear direction and restrained traveling light accents. Inspect the entire travel corridor, including departure and docking, so pulses/glows do not cross labels. A light string must still communicate the actual connection. Alpha silhouettes, occlusion and intentional docking need human triage beyond rectangular bounds.

Karaoke captions stay fixed in screen space, higher than the platform's lower overlays. Measure the selected font, split into at most two readable lines, and highlight the spoken word from measured cues. Choose safe bounds for the actual platform and test the encoded phone copy; one past episode's coordinates are not a universal safe area. Keep captions outside world camera transforms.

Depth polish should retain approved layout, identity, narration, timing and causal operations. Change only the material/thickness/shadow treatment requested, then recheck alpha, crop, readability and transition states. Cache only when actual raster equivalence has been verified, including alpha and all relevant transform/opacity states.
