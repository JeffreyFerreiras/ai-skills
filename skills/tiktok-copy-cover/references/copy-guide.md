# TikTok copy guide

Trigger: caption, copy, cover, or thumbnail is requested for the current
video or topic. Read this guide then; skip it for unrelated work.

## Inputs

Reuse the inspected script, brief, or chat facts. Ask only when a material
fact is absent (topic, audience, link/CTA, required names). Missing media
never counts as an inspected video.

## Deliverables

1. One strong hook/title line.
2. One ready-to-post caption, plain language, no em dashes.
3. Three to five relevant lowercase hashtags (editorial preference, not platform law).
4. One optional pinned comment only when it adds something useful.
5. One real `cover.png` (default PNG 1080x1920) plus an editable SVG when
   the composition is code-native, with clear paths.
6. Short note of what was inspected and what was not.

## Constraints

- Present the title as a plain line and the caption as a separate plain
  paragraph, without labels such as "Title:" or "Description:". Keep
  hashtags with the caption so the copy is ready to paste.
- Ground every claim in inspected facts; never invent timing, coverage,
  engagement, or style-match guarantees.
- Keep internal facts, file paths, and review notes out of posting copy.
- Never present a prompt or description as the cover itself.
- No publishing, login, upload, or social messaging unless explicitly
  requested. Verify expected source account and notebook identity before
  any requested external read or write; never default to another signed-in
  profile or route around a blocked source.
- Do not regenerate video for a copy/cover-only task.
- Cover work follows the bundled cover-style gate in this skill root.
