# Dark drafting desk

Use this optional treatment when the user selects Dark drafting desk or asks for
the dark architect's drafting-desk style. Include its saved frame in the
[visual style picker](style-gallery.md). Selecting it does not replace other
styles or select the style for future episodes.

Before designing, open the [approved reference frame](../assets/style-references/dark-drafting-desk.png).
Compare the actual pixels with the candidate at delivery size. The reference
establishes appearance, not motion quality or narration approval.

## Surface and palette

Use a matte dark slate drafting surface with a subdued blue-gray grid and sparse
registration marks. Keep construction lines behind the mechanism. Draw precise
ivory outlines and level, readable technical labels. Use the following colors as
the reference palette, with contrast checked in the actual encode.

| Role | Reference color |
| --- | --- |
| Drafting surface | `#111c26` |
| Ivory lines and labels | `#d7e3e9` |
| Component faces and grid | `#1b2b37`, `#1c2c38` |
| Copper accent | `#c18d67` |
| Sage accent | `#91afa1` |

Reserve copper and sage for a few meaningful states or objects. Establish their
meaning within the episode and keep it stable across cuts. Pair color with labels
or shapes. Do not infer a universal success, failure or traffic meaning from this
palette. Use dark text on light accent-filled badges when needed for contrast.

## Depth and motion

Give components distinct silhouettes, solid offset side faces and restrained edge
shading. Keep connector endpoints, labels and arrow direction visible. Avoid neon
glow, luminous trails and decorative pulses. Keep the grid stationary so motion
belongs to the event being explained.

Animate the mechanism inside the scene. A message travels along its actual
directed relationship, a receipt emerges after its triggering event, or a record
changes only after the operation completes. These are examples, not a required
payment scenario or scene template. Retain useful anchors and choose a new view
when the teaching question changes. Camera movement alone does not explain a
state change.

Keep captions in the existing safe area, with readable word highlighting. For a
style-only revision, preserve approved narration and timing unless the user also
requests those changes. Apply the skill's existing scene, story-bridge and encoded
review gates. This style reference does not replace the separate cover guidance.
