# Production review

Use the project's current production record. The
[template](../assets/production-record.md) supplies evidence fields when none
exist. Keep technical truth, teaching quality, and visual quality separate.
The spec checker certifies none of them.
Run the [built-in review loop](video-review-loop.md) for each gate. Its state,
candidate binding, same-Writer repair, fresh-Reviewer, and decision rules are
required workflow here, not an optional external loop invocation.

## Scope and evidence

Review the script/storyboard, representative sample, rough full timeline, and
encoded final as they are produced. Review assets and audio/cues before expensive
assembly. Assign a non-author reviewer at every asset deliverable: production
document/script, storyboard/art, narration/cues, representative sample, full
render, and delivery encode. Group related files when each is covered explicitly;
do not create extra exports just to populate a checklist. A plan-only request
ends at the plan.
Obtain independent technical and narrative approval of the current script,
spoken hook, source claims and storyboard before TTS or any rendering. Review
voice consistency before final visual timing, every scene's visual and logical
clarity, then the current encoded delivery. No earlier-stage pass approves a
materially changed later revision.

- Technical: trace claims to inspected passages, distinguish illustrative choices
  from product guarantees, and verify event order, state, labels, cues, and media
  contracts. A finite demonstration does not prove a distributed-systems guarantee.
- Editorial: check orientation, stakes, definitions, mechanism, practical use,
  failures, and scope of the conclusion. Inspect native-paced explanations,
  transitions, holds, and narration/state agreement across the complete lesson.
  Review and lock the spoken hook's accurate promise and immediate payoff before full narration
  lock, and verify that the contextual introduction and ordered roadmap follow.
  Accept question, phrase, or defensible claim without requiring a question mark.
  Apply the hook rubric to specific relevance/interest, evidence, payoff, natural
  delivery, and picture alignment. The 3-6-second heuristic cannot justify rushed
  speech, and ad guidance is not proof of organic retention.
- Voice consistency: use a dedicated independent reviewer before final visual
  retiming. Follow [local narration](local-narration.md#independent-voice-consistency-gate)
  for rushing/dragging, midphrase pauses, identity/timbre/energy, and neighboring
  joins. Record actual listening coverage separately from instrumented checks;
  leave perceived naturalness pending for user audition when listening is absent.
  Verify that a narrated final's actual encoded opening audio contains the hook
  and leads into the explanation; cover text and subtitles alone cannot pass it.
- Visual: cover every scene and boundary transition, including the actual encoded
  final at delivery size. Inspect composition, hierarchy, asset sharpness, labels,
  causal motion, and reference matches. A geometry pass is not an aesthetic pass.

Give any authorized independent reviewer the brief, sources, accepted choices,
exact revision and artifact hashes, stable scene IDs, and actual artifacts.
The author cannot act as its own independent reviewer. Technical and editorial
scopes may share a reviewer with distinct verdicts. Assign a dedicated visual
reviewer, separate from the creator and technical/editorial roles, to every scene.
When delegation is unavailable or unauthorized, record scoped self-check evidence
and leave the independent gate pending. The creator cannot self-approve the script
or clear a dependent production gate.

Keep a scene ledger with scene ID/time range, establishing and mechanism frames,
transition/movement evidence, actual encoded delivery size, round count, findings,
and outcome. Judge composition, focal hierarchy, distinct semantic forms, crisp
assets, authentic reference treatment, purposeful action, polished transitions,
and upright phone-readable labels/captions. Reject static text or camera movement
as the only mechanism, midpoint text doubling, and repeated generic layouts.
Technical correctness cannot certify visual appeal; no scene may be omitted.

Record frame indexes/timecodes, played intervals, listening coverage, and any
missing scenes. Decode checks, sampled images, continuous playback, waveform
alignment, model cues, and independent forced alignment are different evidence.
Do not claim watching or listening from a transcript or contact sheet. Missing
required coverage remains pending; a prior still/sample pass cannot approve a
later encode. At final review, inspect encoded labels and moving details, not
only the renderer's source stills.

For a supplied aesthetic reference, inspect its actual pixels alongside the
candidate. Record reference identity, dimensions, concrete matches/mismatches,
and any access limit. User aesthetic rejection reopens the relevant style finding;
automated checks or numerical beauty scores cannot override it. Preserve other
passes only where their verified inputs and scope are unchanged.

## Fresh-context logical scene review

Use an independent logical scene reviewer distinct from the creator and visual
reviewer. Start with only the context an audience receives: actual full-scene
frames, visible labels, and temporal evidence. Withhold creator reasoning,
intended object identities, and the explanation of how it is supposed to work
until the reviewer records what each important object is, its role, and inferred
flow. Then compare that first reading with intended claims and technical sources.
A plausible first reading alone does not establish domain correctness.

Check recognizable identities, legible support/mounting for physical equipment,
and visible source/destination continuity for every meaningful connector. Follow
paths through entrances, camera moves, crossings, occlusions, and cutbacks; reject
wires that appear from nowhere or objects that read as unrelated devices. Establish
intentional abstraction before showing floating data. Check trigger before
response, travel before arrival, processing before result, and accurate direction
and roles across the whole composition.

Record initial interpretation, intended meaning, concrete mismatch, consequence,
visible repair, scene/time range, and revised evidence. Inspect temporal evidence
spanning the scene and causal boundaries, not only a hero still or source geometry.
State sampled-frame versus continuous-playback coverage precisely. Logical review
adds a responsibility within the existing asset budget; it does not reset rounds
or replace visual, technical, story, or voice review. Unresolved material logical
findings block dependent acceptance and must be reported at the cap.

## Bounded repairs

Use at most three review rounds per identified deliverable stage. Stop at the
first clear round; the initial review counts as round one, with at most two
repair/review cycles. Run the loop's validation and current-digest checks before
each independent review. A missing or malformed decision blocks the pass.
Technical, editorial, logical, visual, and voice scopes share that stage's
counter; reviewer changes, revised files, and renamed exports do not reset it.
Scene findings remain within that asset/stage budget; the dedicated visual role
does not add three extra rounds. Use focused frames/excerpts and targeted renders,
not three obligatory full encodes.
The sample, full timeline, and final encode are distinct stages, each with its
own counter. Carry unresolved findings forward; advancing stages cannot evade a
failed prerequisite. An optional requested copy has a separate encoded review.

For each round, record candidate hash, reviewer/self-review identity, scope,
candidate manifest/digest, role results and stable criterion/finding IDs, plus
validation receipts and consumed round reservations. Self-check identity is
evidence of a self-check only, never an independent approval. Record inspection
coverage, findings, and `APPROVE`, `REVISE`, or `BLOCK`. Each finding needs a stable ID, scene/timecode,
observed defect, impact, severity, focused repair, and verification evidence.
Fix routine defects within authorization, then recheck changed intervals and
neighboring transitions within the remaining rounds. Preserve accepted voice,
timing, layout, and style unless the defect requires changing them.

After round three, stop that loop. Surface unresolved critical defects, material
visual findings, or unmet requirements and keep dependent acceptance pending.
The cap does not imply approval. Minor limitations need an explicit disposition.
A narrow new request may limit the review scope, but retains prior unresolved
findings and consumed rounds. Do not run extra rounds under a new filename.
Material revisions invalidate affected approvals and finding resolutions; obtain
explicit current-digest re-review. An omitted prior finding stays outstanding.

Provide the reviewed sample through the authorized local or delivery route.
Record internal review separately from user feedback. Continue authorized work
without repeated routine approval requests, unless the user reserved that sample
decision or an unresolved material choice blocks progress. Sample/style approval,
full-video acceptance, and publication authority remain separate.
