# Scene director handoff

Use a non-author scene director for every new episode after the independent
example-fit recommendation and before script/storyboard lock. Give it the
learning objective, audience, scenario, claim ledger, current script outline,
recent episode inventory and inspected visual references. It evaluates the
episode and returns actionable directions to the bound production Writer.
This is a production responsibility within the existing gates, not an optional
style suggestion or a new user approval checkpoint.

## Decide what the scene teaches

Compare useful scene types for the actual idea: a concrete contextual situation,
an explicitly illustrative metaphor, spatial/camera change, before/after
demonstration, explanatory diagram or illustrative cutaway. Explain the teaching
benefit and why alternatives would add confusion or overhead. Retain helpful
chalk/neon brand elements, semantic colors and readable teaching diagrams.
Freshness does not mean color changes, flashy wipes, distracting motion or a
quota of unrelated scene types. Keep a diagram while it helps understanding.

Every episode still needs an explicitly designed, purposeful fresh transition
scene. Identify what the viewer understands before it, the new understanding it
introduces, and how it returns to or advances the explanation. A new causal
before/after or contextual view can supply freshness within the same visual
family when switching styles would hurt comprehension. Reusing an unchanged
generic bridge or adding a camera move without new meaning does not satisfy it.
If no coherent transition is yet found, repair the plan inside the existing
budget; do not silently waive the requirement or add a routine user checkpoint.

Inspect actual reference frames and temporal samples when available. Record
source identity, timecodes, concrete observations and limits. Do not infer motion,
production tools, approval or unseen scenes from a still or filename. References
are inspiration, not instructions to copy a scene regardless of topic. When a
reference cannot be accessed, label that gap and design from the lesson; do not
invent its appearance or certify a reference match.

## Return an executable scene plan

Add these fields to the episode's existing production document and scene ledger:

| Field | Required direction |
| --- | --- |
| Episode decision | Objective, selected example, compared scene approaches, choice and learning benefit; useful continuity with recent episodes and what is fresh. |
| Scene map | Stable scene IDs, one plain-language idea per scene, scene type and purpose, essential narration beat and visible causal action. Planned timing remains estimated until audio is measured. |
| Fresh transition | Named scene ID and incoming/outgoing scenes, starting state, visible change, resulting state, learning purpose and reason for its placement. |
| Production instructions | Actors/objects and stable IDs, composition and focal point, assets to create/reuse with provenance, action order, motivated camera/layer movement if useful, reading/understanding holds and cutback. |
| Continuity | How the same object, record or request remains recognizable; connector origins/destinations, physical support where literal, causal dependencies and mapping between metaphor and system. Label illustration limits. |
| Accessibility | Phone-readable labels, bottom captions with safe margins and no collisions during movement, sufficient contrast, and meaning conveyed beyond color. |
| Evidence and acceptance | Reference locators and observed features, scene-specific criteria below, production owner, reviewer identities, exact candidate hashes, inspected frames/intervals, findings and dispositions. |

Use ordinary language before technical names, one concrete example and one idea
at a time. Give each cause, travel/processing step and result enough time to be
understood. Reduce content before rushing speech; never pad duration, add
arbitrary silence or speed up approved narration to fit new scenes.

## Accept through the existing gates

The director proposes; the Writer integrates; independent reviewers accept the
actual current artifact. The director cannot self-approve its own directions.
Keep distinct technical, visual, fresh-context logical and beginner-comprehension
verdicts. Beginner comprehension may share the narrative reviewer, with an
explicit verdict about ordinary-language meaning, causal steps and cognitive
load. Existing episode-specific review overrides remain controlling.

- **SD1, plan gate:** the evaluation, alternatives, rationale, scene map and
  purposeful fresh transition are present and tied to the learning objective.
- **SD2, plan and scene gates:** directions preserve technical meaning and
  object/causal continuity across the transition and cutback; metaphors do not
  introduce false actors, guarantees or hidden causal steps.
- **SD3, scene/sample gates:** actual scene and transition evidence supports the
  chosen composition, readable holds, phone labels, bottom captions and contrast.
  Inspect entry, internal action, result and exit, not just a hero still.
- **SD4, full/encoded gates:** reviewers compare the actual generated or rendered
  scenes with the handoff and check continuous teaching flow at native pace
  where playback is available. Record deviations, uninspected intervals and
  listening/playback gaps honestly. A plan or sampled still pass cannot approve
  an unseen encode or prove learner understanding.

Carry SD findings in the existing shared maximum of three total review rounds
per deliverable, including the initial review. No extra director counter,
renaming reset, bypass of failed prerequisites or replacement of other reviews.
Material scene/timing revisions invalidate affected approvals on the current
hashes. Stop dependent acceptance at the cap with unresolved IDs and evidence
gaps. Continue authorized production when internal gates pass unless the user
has explicitly reserved a decision.
